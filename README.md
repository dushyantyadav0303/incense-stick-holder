## About the Project
### **incense stick holder** - It is 3d printed incense stick holder design in fusion 360. which can use to hold incense stick and the incense coil.


## why i made this?
### I needed a Aesthetic incense stick holder and i Have a 3d printer so i thought why not make our own.
